02 About Us

A little tradition.
A lot of flavour.

Maine Kitchen Africana brings authentic Nigerian food to the heart of Dugbe, Ibadan.

We prepare delicious Nigerian meals using familiar local ingredients and traditional flavours. From rich soups and stews to satisfying rice dishes, every meal is made to be fresh, filling, and enjoyable.

We also provide daily fresh food packs, doorstep delivery, and catering for events and special occasions.
