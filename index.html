Our Story

A taste of home, made with heart.

Maine Kitchen Africana is built around a simple idea: good Nigerian food should be fresh, satisfying and made with care.

From rich Egusi and Banga soups to comforting rice dishes, every meal is prepared to give you that familiar taste of a proper Nigerian kitchen.

Whether you're stopping by for lunch, ordering a fresh food pack, feeding your family or planning an event, we're here to serve you.
